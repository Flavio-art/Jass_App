Privacy Policy

App: Jass Coiffeur & Schieber · Last updated: 24 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the app "Jass Coiffeur & Schieber" (the "App") handles data. Protecting your data matters to us. The App collects no personal data. To improve the App, only anonymous usage statistics that cannot be attributed to any individual are collected (see Section 3).

1. No personal data

The App collects no personal data. No registration and no user account are required. The App does not ask for information such as your name, email address or location. A player name entered in the App stays exclusively on your device and is not transmitted.

2. Local storage

Game states, scores and settings are stored exclusively locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to anyone. Uninstalling the App removes it.

3. Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To improve the App we use Google Firebase Analytics (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA). Only anonymous, aggregated usage data is collected, in particular:

  • which game type is started and finished (e.g. Schieber, Differenzler, Coiffeur),
  • the score achieved in a game,
  • a random, anonymous app-instance identifier and basic technical data (e.g. device model, operating-system version, approximate region).

This data is not linked to your identity, does not allow any conclusions about individual persons and is not used for cross-app tracking or advertising. No advertising identifiers (e.g. IDFA) are used. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in anonymous reach and usage measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / Swiss FADP). More information about Google's processing: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. No advertising

The App contains no advertising and no third-party services other than the anonymous statistics described in Section 3.

5. Share and file features

If you share content within the App or import/export a file, this happens only on your explicit action and through your device's standard functions. No data is transmitted to us in the process.

6. Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and collects no personal data from users of any age.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. Changes are published on this page and the date above is updated accordingly.
