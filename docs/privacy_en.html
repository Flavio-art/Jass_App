Privacy Policy

App: Jass Coiffeur & Schieber · Last updated: 29 August 2026

This privacy policy describes how the app "Jass Coiffeur & Schieber" (the "App") handles data. Protecting your data matters to us. The App is intentionally designed so that no personal data is collected, stored or transmitted to third parties.

1. No data collection

The App collects no personal data. No registration and no user account are required. The App does not ask for information such as your name, email address or location.

2. Local storage

Game states, scores and settings are stored exclusively locally on your device (using the device's local storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to anyone. Uninstalling the App removes it.

3. No internet connection

The App works fully offline. It requests no internet permission and makes no network connections. No data is transmitted to servers or third parties.

4. No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party services that could collect data.

5. Share and file features

If you share content within the App or import/export a file, this happens only on your explicit action and via your device's standard functions. No data is transmitted to us in the process.

6. Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and collects no data from users of any age.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will be published on this page and the date above will be updated accordingly.
