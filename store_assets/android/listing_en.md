## Full description (max. 4000 chars)
Jass Coiffeur & Schieber brings Switzerland's national card game to your phone –
with strong AI opponents, all the important game modes and completely offline.
Whether you want to master the Coiffeur, play a quick Schieber or train your
declaration strategy: play Jass anytime, without internet and without other
players.

GAME MODES
• Coiffeur (Friseur) – as a team or solo with a wish card
• Schieber – the classic with trump and meld (Weis)
• Differenzler – predict your trick points as precisely as possible
• Plus Obenabe, Undenufe, Slalom, Misère, Tutti, Schafkopf and more

STRONG AI
The computer opponents play with sophisticated card logic (Monte Carlo plus
expert heuristics) and challenge you in every mode. Perfect for practising
strategies, replaying tricky situations and improving your game.

YOUR BENEFITS
• 100% offline – no internet, no account, no registration
• No ads, no tracking, no hidden purchases
• French and German card decks
• Scores and games are stored locally on your device
• Clean, fast interface – start playing right away

Note: Traditional Swiss Jass terms (Schieber, Coiffeur, Obenabe, Undenufe,
Slalom, Differenzler …) are kept in their original form.

WHO IS IT FOR?
For everyone who loves Swiss Jass: from beginners learning the rules of each mode
to experienced players sharpening their Coiffeur and Differenzler tactics against
a strong AI.

Download Jass Coiffeur & Schieber now and jass whenever and wherever you like –
completely offline.
